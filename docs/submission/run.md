The fastest way to see it: open [https://stylebook.dev](https://stylebook.dev) and click **Try the demo**. You get your own copy of a sample content team's workspace for 24 hours, signed in as its editor, with suggestions waiting. No account needed.

To use it with a team: click **Start a workspace**, sign in with the code Cloudflare emails you, invite people from People, and connect Claude or Cursor from **Connect your tools**.

To host your own: follow "Run it yourself" in the README. You need Node.js 22 and a Cloudflare account on Workers Paid (Artifacts requires it). `npm install`, create the D1 database and KV namespace, apply migrations, `npx wrangler deploy`.
