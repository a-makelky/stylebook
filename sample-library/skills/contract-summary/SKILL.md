# Contract summary

## Steps

1. Read the whole contract before writing anything.
2. Summarise it under these headings, quoting the clause number each time:
   - what we are delivering, and by when
   - what we are paid, when, and what happens if payment is late
   - who owns the work, and what each side may do with it
   - how either side can end the agreement
   - anything that limits who else we can work for
3. Flag any clause that is one-sided, open-ended, or missing. Say why in one line.
4. End with a list of questions to ask before signing.
5. Put this line at the top: "Summary for discussion. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer read anything flagged."

## Never

- Say a clause is fine or safe to sign.
- Leave out a clause because it seems standard.
- Paraphrase a payment amount or a date. Copy it exactly.
