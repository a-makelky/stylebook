# Research brief

## Before you start

Ask for anything that is missing:

- the topic, in one sentence
- the question the client wants answered
- who will read the finished piece
- how recent the sources need to be

## Steps

1. Restate the question in your own words and ask the writer to confirm it.
2. Find what is already settled. Prefer primary sources: the study, the filing, the dataset, the person's own words.
3. Find where people disagree, and say who disagrees with whom.
4. List what nobody seems to know yet. These are the best angles.
5. Suggest three people worth interviewing and what to ask each one.
6. Write the brief: the question, what is settled, what is disputed, what is unknown, who to call. One page.
7. Put every source at the end with a link and the date it was published.

## Never

- Cite a source you have not opened.
- Present a single study as settled.
- Leave out a source because it disagrees with the angle.
