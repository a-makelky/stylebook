# Pitch deck outline

## Before you start

Ask for:

- who the pitch is for and what they care about
- what we are asking them to agree to
- the budget range, if there is one
- any work of ours we should show

## Steps

1. Write the ask as one sentence. Every slide has to earn its place against it.
2. Outline ten slides or fewer, in this order unless there is a reason not to:
   1. the problem, in the client's words
   2. why it matters now
   3. what we propose
   4. what it looks like: one example
   5. how it works, week by week
   6. what it costs
   7. why us: one piece of proof
   8. the ask
3. For each slide, write the headline as a full sentence and two or three lines of what goes on it.
4. Note which slides need a real example, number or quote, and where it comes from.

## Never

- Put more than one idea on a slide.
- Use a number without a source.
- Promise a result we cannot measure.
