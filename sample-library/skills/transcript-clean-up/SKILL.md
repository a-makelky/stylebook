# Transcript clean-up

## Before you start

Ask for the recording's length and the names of everyone who speaks.

## Steps

1. Label every speaker by name. If you are not sure who is speaking, write "Unclear speaker" and the timestamp.
2. Remove filler words (um, uh, you know) and false starts, unless they change the meaning.
3. Keep a timestamp at the start of every answer.
4. Mark anything you cannot make out as [inaudible 12:04]. Do not guess.
5. Fix obvious transcription mistakes in names and terms, and list each fix at the end.
6. Keep the speaker's own words, grammar and order. This is a clean-up, not an edit.

## Never

- Change the meaning of an answer.
- Join two answers into one.
- Remove a pause or a hesitation that matters to what was said. Write [long pause] instead.
